# DP-001 — Competition Framework

**Working competition name:** AI Architecture Discovery Challenge  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Approved working baseline for public refinement  
**Date:** 2026-09-14

This directory operationalizes **DP-001 — National Competition for Governable AI Architectures**.

The workshop began as a proposal to widen the search for governable AI architectures. Version 0.1 turns that proposal into a concrete competition design that can be criticized, refined, piloted, and eventually used for outreach.

The most important participation change is simple:

> A participant does not need to invent an entire architecture or act as a principal investigator. Contributors can join an existing team, take a bounded work packet, design a test, reproduce a result, or try to falsify a claim.

Competition is therefore one mechanism inside the Workshop, not the only doorway into the work.

## Core rule

> Make explicit claims about an AI architecture, provide enough evidence for others to test them, and reward both the people who demonstrate that the claims are true and the people who rigorously demonstrate that they are not.

The competition is not designed to reward novelty for its own sake. Its purpose is to expand the explored design space and generate comparative evidence about capability, governability, robustness, transparency, efficiency, adaptation, and reproducibility.

## Current boundary

Version 0.1 is a competition-design baseline, not authorization to train frontier systems, pool volunteer compute, publish dangerous exploit details, or claim that any architecture has "solved alignment." The first task is to improve the rules and evaluation design enough to justify a bounded pilot.
